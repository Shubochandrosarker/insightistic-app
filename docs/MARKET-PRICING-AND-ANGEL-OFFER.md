Commercial rules: one-time payment; first 100 completed Angel lifetime memberships or the announced campaign end date; allowances reset monthly; lifetime means access to the purchased tier while Insightistic operates, not every future product or unlimited usage; 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee, no questions asked. The guarantee must be configured in the payment provider, support process, website policy and counsel-reviewed terms before launch.

The $149 Angel Studio tier should be the visual default: it is the easiest fit for a real small agency without forcing the buyer into the maximum tier. Sell a workflow, not a feature pile:

1. Connect WordPress/WooCommerce once.
2. See revenue, customers, products, traffic and site signals together.
3. Understand what changed with explainable AI.
4. Turn evidence into decisions and client-ready reports.
5. Keep source-ready product facts and AI-agent discovery paths consistent.

Do not promise higher Google rankings, AI Overview inclusion, ChatGPT citations, backlinks or traffic. Promise the parts Insightistic controls: connected data, clear evidence, repeatable reporting, source-ready information and support.

## Buyer value and first-impression sequence

The buyer gets a repeatable weekly operating view, fewer context switches, a clearer explanation of important changes, client-ready evidence, published limits, a time-boxed risk reversal, and access to the Angel tier while Insightistic operates.

Use this sequence on /ltd/:

1. Recognition: WordPress and WooCommerce data is spread across too many places.
2. Specific promise: connect it once and understand what changed.
3. Proof surface: show real tier limits, a sample workflow, product/source pages and the support route.
4. Choice architecture: three tiers, with Angel Studio featured.
5. Risk reversal: 30-day full refund, no questions asked.
6. Honest urgency: first 100 Angel members and an announced campaign window; no fake countdown.
7. Low-friction next step: choose a tier or request a fit check.
8. Trust maintenance: linked terms, refund, privacy/security, contact and source guide.

Use clarity and reduced risk, not manipulation. Do not use invented testimonials, fake live counters, fabricated logos, guaranteed outcomes or hidden overages.

## SEO, AIO, AEO, GEO and AI-agent maintenance

Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same foundational SEO practices as ordinary Search; there is no special schema or AI-only file that guarantees inclusion. Keep the site crawlable, internally linked, indexable, textually clear, fast and aligned between visible copy and structured data. Monitor Search Console.

The implementation includes:

- /ltd/ as the canonical offer page;
- /ai-agents/ as a human-readable source and citation guide;
- /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt summaries;
- Product and Offer structured data for Angel tiers;
- FAQ structured data matching visible FAQ copy;
- explicit OAI-SearchBot allowance;
- linked terms, refund, privacy, security, about and contact pages.

OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot from GPTBot and ChatGPT-User. Allowing OAI-SearchBot can make a site eligible for ChatGPT Search discovery; it is not a citation or ranking guarantee.

Maintenance: weekly during the campaign verify cap, prices, checkout, refund route, canonical links and support; monthly refresh the source guide, structured data, competitor comparison and Search Console review; on every product release update source pages, llms files, feature claims, screenshots and terms.

## Launch blockers from the repository audit

Before accepting paid Angel members:

- Configure/test real Paddle one-time prices, checkout, webhooks and refunds.
- Verify the first-100 cap under concurrent checkout and webhook delivery.
- Implement refund/reversal/reconciliation so refunded purchases do not retain incorrect access.
- Configure the 30-day no-questions-asked policy in Paddle and support operations.
- Replace draft terms and refund language with counsel-reviewed documents.
- Run a real WordPress/WooCommerce connection and first sync.
- Verify lifecycle email, trial conversion, billing portal, cancellation and dunning.
- Add rate limiting to auth, license and connector endpoints.
- Complete DNS/SSL, mail delivery, monitoring, queue/cron, backups and privacy/deletion checks.
- Obtain real customer proof before adding testimonials or social proof.
